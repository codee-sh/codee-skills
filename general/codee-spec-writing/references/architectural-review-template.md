# Architectural Review Template

Use this format when the user asks to review or audit a specification. Keep findings evidence-based and point to the affected spec section or repository source.

```md
# Architectural Review: {Spec Title}

## Summary

{Scope, proposed approach, and overall assessment in a short paragraph.}

## Findings

### Critical

- {Finding, evidence, impact, and required correction, or `None`.}

### High

- {Finding, evidence, impact, and required correction, or `None`.}

### Medium

- {Finding, evidence, impact, and required correction, or `None`.}

### Low

- {Finding, evidence, impact, and suggested correction, or `None`.}

## Checklist

| Check | Status | Evidence / justification |
|---|---|---|
| {check} | PASS / FAIL / N/A | {concise evidence} |

## Verdict

`APPROVED` | `CHANGES REQUIRED`

{Required changes when approval is withheld.}
```
